# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in GProxyT welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/w0fv1/gproxyt/security/advisories/new). The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This code applies to project spaces and to public representation of the project. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.
